HotTake is out, and it's free. It turns a Windows laptop into a party-game console, and every phone in the room becomes a controller.

How it works

  1. Install HotTake on a Windows 10 or 11 laptop and put it on the TV.
  2. Everyone scans the QR code on screen with their phone camera.
  3. Their phone turns into the controller for whatever game is on: a joystick and buttons for brawlers, quick taps for board and dice games.

No app to install, no accounts, and no cloud server. The laptop hosts everything over your own Wi-Fi, so it works anywhere you have a router.

51 games in the box

Every game ships inside the installer, for 1 to 8 players. A few favourites:

  • Bluff Cup: hide your dice, raise the bid, call the liar.
  • Beetle Ball: beetles, a seed ball and two flower-pot goals.
  • Night Siege: five nights of co-op survival. Hold the fire, together.
  • Putt Panic, Ricochet Tanks, Lava Rise, Bombermoji and plenty more brawls, races and co-op chaos.

A few games are ports that keep their original licences and credits.

Playing with friends far away

Online play works too, if you set up your own tunnel. The docs walk through it step by step.

Get it

Download HotTake and browse every game at hotake.pranshubasak.site. The how-to-play guide gets you from download to first round in about two minutes.
